Phase 6: The Final Submission Engine
Your student experience is almost 100% complete. They can make an account, fill out their profile, upload documents, log their test scores, write their essay, find colleges, and invite recommenders.

The next logical step is The Submission. The student needs a way to bundle all of this information together, electronically sign it, and officially send it to a university.

Copy and paste this exact prompt into Replit:

"Act as a Senior Full-Stack Developer. We are moving to Phase 6 of our Common App architecture: The Final Submission Engine. We need to allow students to officially submit their completed profiles to the universities in their 'My Colleges' list.

Task 1: Pre-Submission Validation Logic

Create a validation utility that checks if a student is 'Eligible to Submit'.

To be eligible, the student must have: 100% Profile Completion, uploaded Verification Documents, at least one Test Score, and a completed Personal Essay (word count > 250).

Task 2: The 'Review & Submit' Flow

On the 'My Colleges' page, when a student clicks 'Mark as: Review and Submit' for a specific university, open a full-screen modal or a new page.

Step 1 of Modal (Review): Show a read-only summary of the data that will be sent (Profile summary, Essay title, Test scores).

Step 2 of Modal (Payment/Fee): If the selected university has applicationFeeRequired: true, show a mock payment screen (e.g., 'Pay Application Fee via Kuickpay / Debit Card' with a 'Simulate Payment' button). If false, skip this step.

Step 3 of Modal (Signature): A final FERPA/Integrity agreement where the user must type their full legal name as a digital signature.

Task 3: The Application Snapshot (Backend)

Create a new applications table in the database.

When the student completes the final step, send a POST request to /api/applications.

This endpoint must take a 'snapshot' of the student's current profile, essay, and tests (save as JSON in a payload column) so that if the student changes their profile later, the submitted application to this specific university does not change.

Update the user_colleges status for that university to 'submitted'.

Technical Requirements:

On success, show a celebratory 'Confetti' success screen.

Ensure the UI remains clean and consistent with the established design system."
